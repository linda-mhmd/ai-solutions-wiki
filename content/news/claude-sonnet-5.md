Anthropic announced Claude Sonnet 5 on 30 June 2026. The model targets agentic and automation workloads and reaches performance close to Claude Opus 4.8. Anthropic paired the launch with introductory pricing that runs through 31 August 2026.

## What happened

Anthropic released Claude Sonnet 5 on 30 June 2026. The company positioned the model for agentic and automation workloads, and stated that its performance sits close to Claude Opus 4.8.

Introductory pricing is 2 dollars per million input tokens and 10 dollars per million output tokens through 31 August 2026. After that date, pricing moves to 3 dollars per million input tokens and 15 dollars per million output tokens.

## Why it matters for builders

A Sonnet-tier model that approaches Opus-tier quality changes the cost calculus for teams running high-volume automation. Workloads that lean on {{< relref "glossary/agentic-ai" >}} - multi-step tool use, planning, and long task loops - consume large token counts, so the introductory rate directly lowers the cost of iterating on agents before the higher price takes effect.

For teams building on {{< relref "glossary/reasoning-models" >}}, Sonnet 5 offers a middle option: near-Opus results without committing to the top tier on every call. If you are evaluating Claude for production, the window through 31 August 2026 is the moment to benchmark against your own workloads.
