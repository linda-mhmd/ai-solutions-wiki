OpenAI and Anthropic both filed confidential IPO paperwork with US regulators in June 2026. OpenAI submitted its draft S-1 to the SEC and announced the filing publicly, stating that timing remains undecided and may be delayed because "there are things we want to do that are likely easier as a private company." Anthropic filed approximately a week earlier. Both companies are reportedly targeting valuations above $1 trillion.

## Update, 28 October 2026: Anthropic's public S-1

On **26 October 2026** Anthropic announced that it had "filed a registration statement on Form S-1 with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission" for a proposed initial public offering of Class A common stock. In its words: "Anthropic intends to list its Class A common stock on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the symbol 'ANT.'" Goldman Sachs & Co. LLC and Morgan Stanley & Co. LLC are named as underwriters. "The number of shares of Class A common stock to be offered" and "the price range for the proposed offering have not yet been determined", and the registration statement "has not yet become effective", so no shares can be sold yet.

The SEC's EDGAR index lists the filing under **Anthropic, PBC** (CIK 0002130357), Form S-1, filed 26 October 2026, file number 333-299633.

**Financial figures are not repeated here yet.** Press coverage reports revenue, loss, cash and compute-commitment figures from the prospectus, but the SEC did not allow our automated retrieval of the document, and this wiki does not publish financial figures it has only seen second-hand. Read them in the prospectus itself, linked below. The paragraph below that says Anthropic had made no public announcement was true for the June confidential filing and is kept as the record of that stage.

## What happened

OpenAI published a brief announcement confirming it had submitted a confidential S-1 registration statement to the SEC. The company wrote: "We have not decided on timing yet; it may be a while because there are things we want to do that are likely easier as a private company. But it's a complicated set of tradeoffs and this gives us the option to go public sooner if that ends up being best."

Anthropic filed its confidential IPO paperwork approximately a week before OpenAI, according to multiple reports. Anthropic has not made a public announcement about the filing. The company raised a $65 billion Series H at a $965 billion post-money valuation in May 2026, the same month it said run-rate revenue had crossed $47 billion.

Subsequent reporting suggests OpenAI may delay its public debut into 2027, citing market conditions and whether they can support a $1 trillion-plus valuation. Anthropic is reportedly still tracking toward a late-2026 listing.

## Why it matters for builders

These filings do not change what you can build today, but they signal where the industry is headed.

A public offering forces disclosure. Once either company files publicly, builders will have audited financials, revenue breakdowns by segment, customer concentration data, and risk factors that are currently opaque. That information matters if you are building on their APIs and want to understand your vendor's sustainability and incentives.

The valuation targets also reflect how much capital frontier AI now requires. Both companies are raising and spending at a scale that only a handful of organizations can sustain. That concentration shapes who can build frontier models and how pricing and roadmaps will evolve. If you depend on these APIs, keep your integration portable where you can.
