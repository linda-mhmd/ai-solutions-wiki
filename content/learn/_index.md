Five ways to enter the wiki. Each is built for a different starting point and goal. Pick the one that matches where you are, not where you think you should be.

---

## Path 1: Start at Zero

**Who it is for:** Someone with no technical background who wants to understand how technology works from the ground up, ending at production AI systems.

**What it covers:** Five progressive levels. Hardware and networks (Level 0) then code and terminals (Level 1) then version control (Level 2) then databases, APIs, and cloud (Level 3) then AI, LLMs, and vibe coding (Level 4).

**Time investment:** 8-12 hours total. Each level is 2-4 short articles.

**Start:** [Start at Zero](/levels/)

---

## Path 2: AI Foundations (2-hour fast track)

**Who it is for:** Someone who already uses computers and the internet comfortably, but has no AI background. You want to understand AI specifically, not relearn computing from scratch.

**What it covers:** Generative AI then machine learning then large language models then AI agents then hallucination then what to use and when.

**Time investment:** 2-3 hours.
