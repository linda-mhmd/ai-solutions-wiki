## Who you would be working with

This wiki is written and maintained by **[Linda Mohamed](https://www.lindamohamed.com)**, an AWS Community Hero and AI Solutions Architect based in Austria, with close to two decades building production software. Linda works with teams and solo builders across Europe, taking projects from a first prototype to a governed, observable, production-grade system that real people can depend on.

The point of this page is simple: to help you tell when you have reached the line where help pays for itself, and to make getting that help easy and low-pressure.

## Four ways to get help

| Offer | What you get | Best when you are |
|---|---|---|
| **App audit and rescue review** | An honest review of your live app's security, data handling, and architecture, with a prioritised list of what to fix first | In the amber-to-red zone: real users, and unsure what is safe |
| **Advisory and fractional CTO** | Regular calls and a sounding board before the big decisions, from someone who has shipped production systems | Making ongoing technical calls without a senior engineer beside you |
| **Workshops and training** | Hands-on sessions so you and your team understand the ground you are standing on | You want to build capability, not only outsource it |
| **Hands-on build or fix** | Experienced hands on the parts that genuinely need them, while you stay focused on the product | A specific piece is beyond comfortable do-it-yourself |

Not sure which one fits? That is what the first call is for. Most people need less than they fear, and the honest answer is sometimes "you have got this, here are two things to change."
