The AI Solutions Wiki organizes content around five interconnected layers that represent the complete stack for building AI systems:

## Applications

The user-facing layer where AI capabilities are delivered through products, services, and interfaces that solve real-world problems.

## Orchestration

The coordination layer that manages workflows, chains together AI components, handles routing, and ensures reliable execution of complex AI operations.

## Models

The intelligence layer containing foundation models, fine-tuned models, and the capabilities they provide for understanding, generating, and reasoning about information.

## Data

The foundation layer encompassing data collection, storage, processing, and management that feeds and grounds AI systems with accurate, relevant information.

## Infrastructure

The operational layer providing compute, networking, deployment, monitoring, and the underlying systems that keep AI applications running reliably at scale.
