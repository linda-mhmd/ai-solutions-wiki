At AWS Hero Summit Seattle 2025, Linda Mohamed asked the people around her one question: if you were an AWS service, which one would you be, and why?

The answers say a lot about the services. One answer picks S3 because it is simple. One picks Lambda because it can burst into explosion. One picks IAM because everyone needs it and nobody thinks about it. In the video, a short explainer after each answer, narrated by Linda (her cloned voice), says what the service does and why the answer fits.

These pages keep the answers and the explanations and leave out the people: each part quotes what was said, without names, shows the explanation from the film with the people cut out, and explains the joke in writing. To see everyone answer, watch the full video on Linda's YouTube channel.

The six parts follow the service groups of the film. You do not need to know AWS to follow along. Start with Part 1, or pick the group you are curious about.
