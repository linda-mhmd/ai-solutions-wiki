- `notext` = pure photography, no baked-in text. Use as cinematic backgrounds or mood images.

## Usage rules

**No-text images (cinematic backgrounds):**
Use inside `.bz-figure` or as full-bleed hero sections. Match image to concept:
- Machines, gears, cables = infrastructure, backend, systems
- Silhouettes facing systems = architecture, oversight, strategy
- Groups watching = stakeholder review, governance, team dynamics
- Circuits, PCB = model internals, embeddings, AI hardware
- Furnace, forge, anvil = training, transformation, hard engineering

**Text images (infographics, slide exports):**
Embed with `<figure class="bz-figure">`. No text above the image. Caption below only.
```html
<figure class="bz-figure">
  <img src="/img/slides/scaling-ai-enterprise-infographic-text.png"
       alt="Enterprise AI scaling: pipeline-centric, data-centric asset reuse, experience layer, orchestration and foundation models, governance." loading="lazy">
  <figcaption>Full enterprise AI architecture from data foundation to user-facing experience layer.</figcaption>
</figure>
```
