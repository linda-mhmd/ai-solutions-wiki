Where things stand

The current flagships, and what their spec sheets leave out

Context windows and prices are easy to find anywhere. The last column is the part that takes reading the release notes - and the part that changes what you should actually do.

ModelReleasedContext$/MTok in / outWhat the specs leave out
GPT-6 Astra 3 Sep 2026 1,050,000 $10 / $50 The first model a lab has classified as crossing its own "Critical" cyber threshold. Prompts over 272k tokens bill at 2x input, 1.5x output.
Claude Fable 5.1 1 Sep 2026 1,000,000 $10 / $50 New tokenizer: roughly 30% more tokens for the same text, so 1M tokens is about 555,000 words, not 750,000. Since 22 September, Opus 5.5 claims roughly Fable-level results at $4 / $20.
Muse Spark 1.3 2 Sep 2026 1,000,000 $1.25 / $4.25 Meta's flagship, and not a Llama. Closed weights. A separate Contributor endpoint drops to $0.10 / $0.20 if you let Meta train on your prompts.
Nova 2 Lite 2 Dec 2025 1,000,000 $0.30 / $2.50 The only generally available model in the Nova 2 family. Pro and Omni are still preview. That price is US East with global cross-region routing; geo routing costs 10% more, and Frankfurt about 30% more.
Mistral Large 3 2 Dec 2025 256,000 $0.50 / $1.50 The cheapest frontier-class option here, and the only one whose weights you can download - Apache 2.0, including the flagship. Smallest context window of the five.
